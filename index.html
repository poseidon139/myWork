FLAGSHIP LEAD PROJECT · DISTRIBUTED ARCHITECTURE

Roam The City

The Problem

Multiplayer spatial applications often degrade in performance as hundreds of users send continuous movement updates across an unpartitioned global socket room.

Architecture & Technical Decision

Engineered a localized zone-partitioning model using Socket.IO rooms, Node.js, and PostgreSQL with stateless JWT tokens. Containerized with Docker and orchestrated with Kubernetes to enable horizontal pod autoscaling under user surges.

Verified Result & Lesson

Guaranteed sub-50ms message sync and zero-drop player handoffs across adjacent geographical sectors without overloading client memory.

FLAGSHIP AI PROJECT · REAL-TIME GROUNDING

PodLens (CiteCast)

The Problem

Long-form video podcasts are the world's most consumed educational medium, yet they frequently spread unverified assertions, misrepresented statistics, and animal studies falsely generalized to humans without live citations.

Architecture & Technical Decision

Architected an asynchronous streaming verification engine on Google Cloud Run using Pub/Sub for 30s audio chunking and Gemini 1.5 Flash with Google Search Grounding to distinguish banter from empirical claims. Synchronized timecoded claim cards over WebSockets directly to the video player timeline.

Verified Result & Lesson

Delivered sub-2s claim extraction and verification with primary academic literature citations (DOIs, PubMed) and automated 91%+ credibility dossiers across 60+ minute podcast episodes.
